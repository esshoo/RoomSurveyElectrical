# v1.9.14 Build 32 — Wall photos foundation

This is the first of three deliberately small, device-testable releases for the photographic wall system.

## iPhone test checklist

1. Open an existing or new scan and confirm the bottom selector contains **2D / 3D / الصور**.
2. Open **الصور** and verify every wall has a separate elevation card.
3. Open a wall, rename it and save; close/reopen the scan and verify the name persists.
4. Choose a fixed colour and verify the wall card and 3D wall overlay use it.
5. Import a photo and verify electrical symbols and openings remain above it in the wall card.
6. Press **الذهاب إلى الحائط في 3D** and verify the 3D camera focuses the wall.
7. Hide **صور وألوان الجدران** from layers and verify the original 3D model remains visible.
8. Export/import the full `.3eroom` package and verify wall names/photos persist.

Automatic photographic capture and adaptive segmentation are intentionally not included in this build.
