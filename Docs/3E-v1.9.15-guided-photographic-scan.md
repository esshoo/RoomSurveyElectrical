# 3ERoomElectrical — Guided photographic scan

## Capture strategy

- Each planar wall returned by RoomPlan is treated as an independent photographic surface.
- The surface is divided into adaptive rectangular cells, targeting about 1.35 m × 1.20 m per cell.
- Grid dimensions are capped to avoid unbounded image counts.
- A photo is captured only for the active cell and only when tracking, framing, projected size, viewing angle and camera stability pass validation.
- The AR guide overlay is hidden before the screen image is captured.
- The four projected cell corners are perspective-corrected with Core Image and excess image area is removed.
- The saved image references exactly one segment ID and carries a capture quality score in segment metadata.

## Detected protrusions and corners

RoomPlan describes walls as planar wall snapshots. Any protrusion, column face, recess side or angled return represented by RoomPlan as a separate wall plane is therefore segmented and photographed independently. This release does not invent geometry that RoomPlan did not detect and does not run a separate dense photogrammetry/mesh reconstruction pipeline.

## Data compatibility

New fields on `RoomProject` are optional:

- `wallPhotoSegments`
- `photographicScanProgress`

Older projects decode without migration. Existing wall-photo assets, electrical points, lighting, takeoff and exporters are not changed.
