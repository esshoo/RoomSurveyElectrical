# 3E v1.9.21 — Wall Photo Composite Quality

## Purpose

Improve the visual continuity and diagnostic quality of wall-photo composites while keeping every captured source image unchanged.

## Quality assessment

Each captured tile receives a combined score based on:

- wall-facing and projected-size score from the photographic scanner;
- average exposure;
- clipped shadows and highlights;
- luminance contrast;
- lightweight edge-detail sampling.

Scores below the weak threshold are shown in orange. Missing tiles remain blue and acceptable tiles remain green.

## Selective recapture

The Photos tab can mark weak tiles for recapture. A marked tile keeps its current image and remains visible in the existing composite. On the next photographic scan it becomes eligible for capture again. A successful replacement updates the tile link while retaining the previous source photo in the project.

## Composite rebuild

The manual rebuild action:

1. analyses captured tile colours;
2. calculates a quality-weighted reference colour;
3. applies conservative per-channel normalisation;
4. draws each tile as an opaque base;
5. adds a feathered overlapping pass at internal tile edges;
6. writes a new composite and thumbnail atomically.

The original tile images are never overwritten.

## Performance

- A shared `CIContext` is reused.
- Quality analysis samples a 48×48 image.
- Segments are processed inside autorelease pools.
- Prepared images are resized to their destination tile dimensions before colour correction.
- The composite resolution continues to follow the selected performance profile.
