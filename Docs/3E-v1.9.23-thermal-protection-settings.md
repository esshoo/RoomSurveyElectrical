# v1.9.23 — Configurable thermal protection

## Scope

- Thermal policy selection for RoomPlan sessions.
- Resume stability countdown.
- Optional on-screen thermal status.
- Start prevention and mandatory critical-state stop.

## Policies

| Policy | Fair | Serious | Critical | Resume threshold |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Early | Save/pause after 20 seconds if sustained | Save/pause | Save/pause | Nominal |
| Balanced | Continue monitoring | Save/pause | Save/pause | Fair or nominal |
| Extended | Continue monitoring | Continue with warning | Save/pause | Fair or nominal |

The app cannot access a Celsius temperature. It consumes `ProcessInfo.thermalState`, which iOS determines for the current device and conditions.

## Persistence

The settings are stored as optional fields in `ElectricalPlacementSettings`, preserving backward decoding. Defaults are balanced protection, a 30-second resume stability interval, and visible thermal status.
