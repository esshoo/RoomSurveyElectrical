# v1.9.18 — Performance, quality, and scan scope

## Settings

The App settings page now stores two backward-compatible optional values in `ElectricalPlacementSettings`:

- `spatialScanPerformanceProfileValue`
- `spatialScanContentModeValue`

Older settings decode with the computed defaults:

- `balanced`
- `completeRoom`

## Performance profiles

### Power saving

- 24 FPS photographic and 3D presentation targets.
- Lower photographic evaluation frequency.
- 2048 px captured-square maximum.
- 1536 px wall-composite maximum.
- No SceneKit antialiasing.

### Balanced

- 30 FPS targets.
- Existing thermally throttled evaluation behaviour.
- 3072 px captured-square maximum.
- 2048 px wall-composite maximum.
- 2x SceneKit antialiasing.

### High quality

- Up to 60 FPS when thermal state permits.
- Faster candidate evaluation and stricter wall-facing/stability requirements.
- 4096 px captured-square maximum.
- 3072 px wall-composite maximum.
- 4x SceneKit antialiasing.

Serious or critical thermal state still lowers evaluation/display-link rates regardless of the selected profile.

## Architecture-only mode

When `SpatialScanContentMode.architectureOnly` is selected:

- `RoomProject.objects` is saved as an empty array.
- Raw `CapturedRoomData` is not retained or encoded.
- The processed diagnostic JSON uses the stable architecture summary rather than the full RoomPlan object graph.
- The furniture layer is forced off in the app viewer when the project has no saved furniture.
- App-controlled 2D, 3D overlays, takeoff, GLB, PNG, PDF, and DXF logic sees no furniture records.

The RoomPlan framework may still perform internal object recognition during the live scan because its public `RoomCaptureSession.Configuration` does not expose an object-detection toggle.

## Unchanged

- RoomPlan walls, floors, doors, windows, and openings remain saved.
- Electrical placement rules and design modes are unchanged.
- Manual wall-photo imports remain at their previous maximum quality unless a photographic profile is explicitly supplied.
