# Build 45-HF1 — Wall photo orientation correction

## Problem

Wall openings, electrical points, lights, furniture overlays, and wall-photo textures could appear mirrored left-to-right relative to the captured wall image.

## Fix

- `WallAppearance` now stores migration-safe horizontal and vertical photo-coordinate corrections per wall.
- Horizontal correction defaults to enabled for existing and new projects to fix the reported RoomPlan/photo direction mismatch.
- The wall-photo screen exposes **قلب أفقي** and **قلب رأسي** before the 2D/3D navigation actions.
- **استعادة التصحيح التلقائي** restores horizontal-on and vertical-off.
- The same mapping is used by the photo preview overlays, 2D photo ribbon, 3D SceneKit texture coordinates, and GLB texture coordinates.
- Original photo files and RoomPlan geometry are never rewritten.

## Device acceptance

1. Open a wall with a door and adjacent switch on a known side.
2. Verify the door, switch, wall light, and furniture overlays match the image.
3. Toggle horizontal and vertical correction independently.
4. Save, close, and reopen the project; verify both settings persist.
5. Verify the same orientation in 3D and an exported GLB.
6. Verify a Build 44/45 project migrates without losing photos.
