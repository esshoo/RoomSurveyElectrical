# 3ERoomElectrical v1.9.13 — Interactive Takeoff

## Scope

This release expands takeoff inspection without changing placement or export logic.

## Takeoff details

- Floor and ceiling width, depth and area.
- Wall width, height, gross area, opening deduction and net area.
- Door, window and architectural opening dimensions and area.
- Furniture width, depth and height.
- Electrical groups separated by type and Existing/Proposed status.
- Individual electrical point height, wall number, wall-start distance, nearest-door distance and standard-height comparison.
- Ceiling lights separated into Camera As-Built, Manual 2D and Automatic 2D sources.
- Individual ceiling-light height, diameter, brightness and plan coordinates.

## Plan focus

Selecting an individual electrical point or ceiling light opens a read-only 2D plan. The view:

1. enables the relevant layers;
2. centres and zooms to the selected item;
3. draws an orange/red pulsing marker;
4. keeps the pulse active until the first plan interaction;
5. permits pan, zoom and rotation but hides editing tools.

## Protected systems

The following files and behaviours remain unchanged:

- electrical placement modes and smart rules;
- post-scan camera ceiling-light placement;
- manual and automatic 2D ceiling-light placement;
- project data models and migration behaviour;
- DXF, PDF, PNG and GLB exporters;
- WidgetKit source and signing configuration.
